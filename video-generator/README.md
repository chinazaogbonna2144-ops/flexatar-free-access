# The Girl Who Heard Her Dead Mother

A GitHub Actions project that generates a 15-second, vertical 9:16 mystery short and publishes the MP4 as a downloadable workflow artifact.

## Generate the video
1. Open the repository's **Actions** tab.
2. Select **Generate Mystery Video**.
3. Tap **Run workflow** and run it on the `video-generator` branch.
4. Open the finished workflow run and download **the-girl-who-heard-her-dead-mother-mp4** under Artifacts.

The video is rendered from original, procedurally illustrated scenes with animated camera movement, title cards, and an eerie synthesized soundtrack. It does not require paid APIs, API keys, or third-party stock footage.

## Story beats
- 0–3s: A lonely house beneath a cold moon.
- 3–7s: A girl hears a voice from the dark hallway.
- 7–11s: An old phone lights up with a call from “MOM”.
- 11–15s: The final chilling reveal: “SHE DIED THREE YEARS AGO.”

## Output
`the-girl-who-heard-her-dead-mother.mp4` — 450×800, 15 seconds, H.264 video with AAC audio.
